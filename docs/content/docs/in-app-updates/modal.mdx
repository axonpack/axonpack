This page shows the prompt in each state, so you know what your users will see. You need it when you
want to check that the default prompt is good enough before you change anything.

`<UpdatePrompt />` renders nothing while the app is up to date. When an update is due, it opens over
the current screen.

## A soft update

A **soft update** is one people can postpone.

![Soft update](/in-app-updates/screenshots/soft.png)

*Look at the three actions: Update (the big button), Later and Skip this version.*

| Part                    | What it does                                                           |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Update                  | Opens the store. A spinner shows until the store has opened             |
| Later                   | Hides the prompt until the app next starts                              |
| Skip this version       | Hides it until a newer version comes out. See [Force and skip](/docs/in-app-updates/force-updates) |

## Release notes

When the store gives release notes, a **What's new** row appears. It is underlined and starts closed.
Tap it to open the notes, and tap again to close them. Set `notesExpanded` on `<UpdatePrompt />` to
start it open.

![What's new, open](/in-app-updates/screenshots/expanded.png)

*Look at the chevron and the notes box under What's new.*

The App Store gives notes. Google Play does not, so on Android the row is missing.

![No release notes](/in-app-updates/screenshots/minimal.png)

*With no notes, the row is not shown at all.*

## A forced update

A **forced update** is one people cannot postpone.

![Forced update](/in-app-updates/screenshots/forced.png)

*Look at the red badge and the single Update button. Later and Skip are gone.*

On Android the back button does nothing. An update is forced when the installed version is below the
`minimumVersion`, or when your `required` rule says so. See
[Force and skip](/docs/in-app-updates/force-updates).

## Dark mode

![Dark mode](/in-app-updates/screenshots/dark.png)

*Look at the dark card. The same prompt, with the dark colours.*

The prompt follows the phone's light or dark setting. To pin it, use `theme={{ mode: 'dark' }}` or
`'light'`. If your app sets `userInterfaceStyle` to `"light"` in `app.json`, the phone always reports
light to your app, so the prompt stays light.
